We score every review for sincerity (0–100) instead of just averaging stars.

Signals we look at: length & substance, personal experience markers, superlative density, generic phrases, rating–tone match, and reviewer history.

Places with higher average sincerity scores get boosted in the ranking. This helps surface spots locals actually love instead of review-farmed places.

Ranking always uses your current location (or the ZIP you entered) + the radius you select (2 / 5 / 10 / 20 miles). Only places inside that radius are ranked; the top 5 are shown.

Talk of the Town is a separate top-10 list ranked by verified public star ratings and review counts (Bayesian-weighted, so a 5-star place with only a few reviews does not automatically beat a well-reviewed 4.7). In Brandon / Valrico these scores were last checked November 10, 2026. We never invent ratings — outside that area the tab says coming soon until a live review API is connected.

Want the location? Tap the blue Show on map button on any result card. The map appears with that spot + your location. Map is hidden by default so the rankings stay front and center.
